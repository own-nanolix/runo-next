What Runo is

Runo is a financial control layer designed for modern teams. It connects accounts, payments, and financial activity into one clear operational system.

What enables

Runo gives teams real-time visibility over cash flow, outgoing activity, and commitments. It helps users understand what is happening and what to do next.

Control Layer

Runo is a centralized financial control layer that unifies cash, operations, and decision-making into a single system.

How it works

Runo structures financial data into clear flows and supports execution through AI assistance.

Why it matters

Financial work is often fragmented. Runo reduces complexity and supports faster decisions.
