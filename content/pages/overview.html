A new standard for financial control, meet Runo.

Product Strategy, Research, Visual Design

Runo is a financial control platform designed to reduce the cognitive load teams face when managing cash, payments, and operations. It brings structure to fragmented workflows, enabling faster, more confident decisions across the entire financial system.

By connecting accounts, analyzing real-time activity, and guiding execution through an AI copilot, Runo transforms complexity into clarity — helping teams stay in control, optimize resources, and act with precision.
